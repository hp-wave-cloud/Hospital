About Our Hospital

24/7 Healthcare Support

With more than 21 years of experience in healthcare, Sri Lakshmi Multi Speciality Hospital has been committed to delivering reliable, accessible and compassionate medical care. The hospital provides 24/7 healthcare support with a dedicated emergency ward, supported by polite, skilled and experienced doctors who focus on patient comfort, safety and timely treatment.

The hospital is designed to provide a convenient experience for patients and visitors. Combining modern facilities, emergency care and personalised attention, Sri Lakshmi Multi Speciality Hospital continues to serve the community with dedication and responsibility.
